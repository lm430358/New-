Got a fake 1-star review? Here's what Google actually removes 👇 Save this.

Google only removes reviews that break its policies, not reviews you just disagree with. That includes reviews that aren't based on a real experience, reviews from a competitor or a current or former employee, paid or discounted reviews, and harassment, hate or private info.

To report: Business Profile » Read reviews » Report » pick the reason » Send. Check the status in Google's Reviews Management Tool. It usually takes several days, and if no violation is found you can appeal once.

Either way, reply politely. Future customers read your reply. Follow for more.

Stop chasing payments after the clean. Set it up BEFORE 💳 Save this.

1. Take a deposit or a card on file when they book
2. Put it in writing: payment is due the day of the clean
3. Send the invoice + pay link before you leave
4. Late? Send ONE friendly text: "Hi Sarah, today's invoice is still open. Here's the link to pay. Thank you!"
5. Only charge a late fee if it was in your policy before they booked

You're not begging to get paid. You're running a business. Follow for Part 2: the cancellation policy text.
