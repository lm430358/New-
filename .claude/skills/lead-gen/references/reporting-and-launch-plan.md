# Reporting and the Seven-Day Launch Plan

## Run report (save to `leads/<slug>/runs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`)

```markdown
# <Business> lead-gen run, YYYY-MM-DD

## Summary
- Prospects researched: N
- Qualified leads (new / existing updated): N / N
- High-priority opportunities: N
- Most promising channels: ...

## Top prospects
| Rank | Prospect | Score | Channel | Why (evidence) | Sources |

## Outreach
- Drafts prepared: N
- Awaiting approval: <list>
- Messages actually sent: N (or "none")
- Responses received: <only if visible in a connected system>

## Retargeting and ads
- Audiences identified: ...
- Campaigns actually created or launched: N (or "none")
- Performance: <real data only, with source and date range>

## Status of actions
- Research done: ...
- Recommendations (not yet acted on): ...
- Approved actions: ...
- Completed actions: ...
- Unverified outcomes: ...

## Next actions
1. ...
```

Never fabricate metrics, responses, completed tasks or conversions.

## Seven-day launch plan (for every new business)

- **Day 1:** research the business, market, offer and ideal customer.
- **Day 2:** identify and compare customer segments and acquisition channels.
- **Day 3:** find and qualify an initial batch of prospects.
- **Day 4:** prepare personalized email, Instagram DM and other outreach drafts.
- **Day 5:** review approved outreach results where available; identify retargeting requirements.
- **Day 6:** prepare ad audiences, creative concepts and campaign tests.
- **Day 7:** review results, identify the strongest opportunities, propose the next experiment.

Adapt it to the business and the tools available. Never promise results within seven days.
