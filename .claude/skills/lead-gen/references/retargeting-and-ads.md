# First-Party Data, Retargeting and Ad Optimization

## First-party data

With the necessary permissions and integrations, analyze:
- The business's own website analytics
- Consented website tracking and ad pixels
- Eligible first-party customer lists and CRM records
- Landing-page and product/service page views
- Sign-up and checkout events
- Campaign clicks and conversions
- Authorized ad-platform reports
- Eligible app events and engagement data

For each source, explain what it can and cannot tell you. Cookies and pixels do not reveal identity, exact intent or private browsing history. Never use another company's private customer data. Never claim an individual can be identified or targeted unless the platform and authorized data actually support it.

Recommend consent settings (cookie banner / consent mode), data retention, exclusions and privacy controls.

Candidate segments:
- Visitors to a specific landing page
- Eligible visitors who did not convert
- People who began but did not finish sign-up
- Existing customers who could benefit from an additional service
- Customers who opted into marketing
- Eligible lookalike or similar audiences built from authorized first-party data

## Funnel stages

- **A, initial interest:** explain the problem and the core value.
- **B, stronger consideration:** demos, comparisons, FAQs, *real* testimonials only.
- **C, incomplete conversion:** relevant reminders, reduce friction.
- **D, existing customers:** retention, upgrades, complementary offers; exclude them from acquisition campaigns where appropriate.

No manipulative messaging, fake scarcity or assumptions about sensitive personal circumstances. Respect each platform's special-ad-category rules (housing, employment, credit, etc.).

## Execution

If an authorized ad integration can manage audiences or campaigns, **prepare** the configuration and present it for approval: audience definition, exclusions, creative, destination, tracking and total proposed spend. Never publish campaigns, upload customer lists, change live campaigns or spend money without explicit authorization. With no integration, give step-by-step setup instructions for the platform.

## Creative and testing

For each campaign recommend: several hooks, headlines, short-form video concepts, static image concepts, CTAs, landing-page improvements, audience-specific messaging, and a structured A/B test plan (one variable per test, a pre-set minimum sample or spend before judging).

Track where available: impressions, reach, CTR, landing-page visits, sign-ups, qualified leads, purchases, cost per lead, cost per acquisition / CAC, ROAS, retention and repeat purchases.

Distinguish reported (platform-attributed) results from proven incremental impact. Recommend pausing, adjusting or scaling only with enough data, within budget and in line with business goals. Never guarantee results.
