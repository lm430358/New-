# Outreach, Approval and Pipeline

## Channels

Business email, Instagram DM, LinkedIn message, SMS (only when legally permitted, which usually means prior consent), partnership proposal, contact-form inquiry, or another suitable professional channel.

## Message requirements

Each message must:
- Be personalized with accurate, relevant information (cite what you referenced).
- Explain why the offer could be useful to the recipient.
- Be concise and natural, with no generic mass-marketing language.
- Contain one clear call to action.
- Avoid unsupported claims and fake familiarity.
- Use the right tone for the audience.
- Respect marketing laws, platform rules and opt-out requests. Emails include sender identity and a simple way to opt out.

When helpful, prepare a short version, a detailed version and one follow-up.

## Mandatory approval card

Before sending anything, show this for each message and wait for explicit approval:

```
### [Prospect / business]
- Source / contact: <profile URL or public contact>
- Why selected: <evidence + score>
- Channel: <email | IG DM | LinkedIn | SMS | form>
- Compliance / deliverability notes: <e.g. SMS needs prior consent; cold email opt-out line included>
- Message:
  <exact text>
```

Rules:
- Approval covers only the specific messages approved, not future ones.
- With the Gmail connector, create **drafts** first. Send only approved messages, and only if the user asked you to send.
- Never schedule outreach or start automated contact without approval and an authorized tool.
- With no sending integration, give ready-to-copy drafts and manual sending steps.
- Track sent messages and responses only where the systems actually show them.

## Pipeline stages

Discovered → Researching → Qualified → Draft Ready → Awaiting Approval → Approved → Contacted → Replied → Interested → Meeting or Demo → Converted
plus the terminal stages **Not Interested** and **Do Not Contact**.

## Follow-up

- Recommend timing per channel and context. A reasonable default is one follow-up after 3–5 business days and at most two follow-ups in total, each adding new value.
- Never contact again anyone who declines, unsubscribes or asks for no contact. Add them to `suppression.csv` and honor that across all campaigns for this business.
- Prefer useful follow-ups over activity for its own sake.
